Delete your Aura Market account

Aura Market by Fathomstack

Fastest: in the app

  1. Open Aura Market.
  2. Tap the ⚙ settings button (top right).
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm with Delete forever.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app: by email

Email saadghaffar97@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Aura Market account" and include:

  • your ticker (for example $ALI) and crew name, and
  • the email you added to the app, if you added one.

We confirm and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner. We may ask a question to make sure the request comes from the account owner.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (guest ID and email, if any)
  • Your tickers, price history, Clout balances and holdings in every crew
  • Aura Events you posted, and your votes
  • Your reports and blocks

Friends who held shares in you are paid out in Clout at the current price before your ticker is removed. If you created a crew, it passes to the longest-standing member.

What we keep

Nothing linked to you. Backup copies are overwritten within 30 days. Security logs kept by our hosting provider expire on their normal short schedule.
