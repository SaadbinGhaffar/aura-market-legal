Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · App: Aura Market (Android package com.fathomstack.auramarket) · Developer: Fathomstack

The short version. Aura Market is a play-money game for friend groups. We collect only what the game needs to work: your name and ticker, what you do in your crews, and your email if you choose to add one. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and you can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, payment details, advertising ID or browsing history. Our hosting provider keeps standard security logs (such as IP address and time of request) for a short period to protect the service.

Who can see it

Everything in a crew is visible only to the members of that crew: your name, ticker, price, posts, and how votes on posts are going. Your email, your own votes, reports and blocks are never shown to other players. Crews are invite-only, joined with a 6-character code.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your data and there are no ads. We use these service providers only to run the app:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and hosting.
  • Google (Gmail): sends the login-code emails, only if you add an email.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed straight away from the live database. Copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your tickers, Clout, holdings, votes and posts in every crew, plus your login. Friends who hold shares in you are paid out first. You can also request deletion by email.

Your choices and rights

You can change your display name per crew, leave out your email, report or block anyone, and delete your account at any time. To ask for a copy of your data or to correct it, email us at saadghaffar97@gmail.com.

Age

Aura Market is for people aged 16 and older and is not directed at children. If you believe a child under 16 is using the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules ensure players can only read their own crews and their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Play money

Clout is a play currency with no real-world value. It can't be bought, sold, exchanged or cashed out.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app for important changes.
