Crew rules

Everyone agrees to these before using Aura Market. They are our terms of use.

🎮 Play money only. Clout has no real value. It can't be bought, sold or cashed out.

🫶 Roast, don't hurt. No bullying, harassment, threats or hate. Post things the person would laugh at too.

🔒 Keep private stuff private. No addresses, phone numbers, secrets, or photos and screenshots of people without their OK.

🔞 Nothing sexual or illegal. Keep it clean enough to show your little cousin.

🚩 Report and block. Report posts that cross the line and block anyone you want gone. Reported posts get hidden, and crew creators can remove any post in their crew.

🎂 16 or older. Aura Market is for people aged 16 and up.

What happens when rules are broken

Posts with slurs are blocked automatically. A post is hidden for everyone once 2 people report it, or 30% of the crew in bigger crews. We may remove accounts that keep breaking the rules. To report a serious problem, email saadghaffar97@gmail.com.
